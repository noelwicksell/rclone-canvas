Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026.

rclone-canvas is a personal tool used only by its owner. It requests a single Google permission, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. That permission lets it create, update and read only the files the tool itself uploads to the owner's Google Drive. It cannot see any other files in Drive.

  • What data it handles: only the files it uploads, which are the owner's own course notes.
  • Where the data goes: only to the owner's own Google Drive. Nothing is sent to any other server, sold or shared.
  • Tracking: none. No analytics, cookies or advertising.
  • Credentials: the Google access token is stored only on the owner's computer.
  • Revoking access: access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
